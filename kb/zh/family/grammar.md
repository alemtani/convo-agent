# Grammar — Family (家人)

Minimal patterns to count a family, give ages, and say what people do. Each
pattern uses only `vocab.md` words.

## 1. Counting a family — 家有…口人

家 takes the measure word 口 for people. The count sits between 有 and 口.

- 我家有五口人。 — There are five people in my family.
- 你家有几口人？ — How many people are in your family?

几 asks for a small number (under ten). Use 多少 for larger counts.

> Common learner error: dropping 口 → ✗ 我家有五人. A family count needs 口.

## 2. Having a sibling — 有 / 没有 + 个

For a person rather than a whole family, use 个.

- 我有一个弟弟。 — I have a younger brother.
- 我有两个姐姐。 — I have two older sisters.
- 我没有哥哥。 — I don't have an older brother.

两 replaces 二 before a measure word. 有 is negated by 没, never by 不.

## 3. Asking an age — 多大

多 + an adjective asks "how …?". 多大 asks how old.

- 你哥哥多大？ — How old is your older brother?
- 他今年二十岁。 — He is twenty this year.

There is no 是 in the answer. The number plus 岁 is the whole predicate.

> Common learner error: ✗ 他是二十岁. Ages take no 是.

## 4. Listing people — 和 and 都

和 joins the **last** two items only. 都 ("all") goes before the verb.

- 爸爸、妈妈和我。 — Dad, Mum and me.
- 他们都是学生。 — They are all students.

## 5. Jobs — 做什么工作 / 是 + noun

- 你爸爸做什么工作？ — What does your dad do?
- 他是老师。 — He is a teacher.
- 她是医生。 — She is a doctor.

Identity uses 是 (unlike the age pattern above).

## 6. Bouncing the question back — 呢

Noun/pronoun + 呢 repeats the question without repeating the sentence.

- 你妈妈呢？ — And your mum?
- 我家有三口人。你呢？ — There are three people in my family. And you?

## 7. Possession — 的, and when to drop it

的 marks possession, but family words after a pronoun drop it.

- 我妈妈 — my mum (not ✗ 我的妈妈 in speech)
- 老师的女儿 — the teacher's daughter

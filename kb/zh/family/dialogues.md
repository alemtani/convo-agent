# Seed dialogues — Family (家人)

Short exchanges that feed the session **sketch** (per `DESIGN.md`). They model
the arc — how many people → who they are → ages → what they do — using only
`vocab.md` words. Lines are short on purpose (beginner disfluency mitigation:
the partner asks questions expecting 2–4 word answers).

Each line shows 汉字 then **pinyin** (the same format the partner reply uses).
Pinyin is generated by `_tools/annotate_pinyin.py` from the curated `vocab.md`
readings, so it always agrees with the vocabulary table (neutral tones, 不/一
sandhi). Regenerate after editing a line.

## Dialogue 1 — How many people, and how old

> **A:** 你好！你家有几口人？
> _nǐ hǎo! nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ kǒu rén?_
> **B:** 我家有五口人。
> _wǒ jiā yǒu wǔ kǒu rén._
> **A:** 都有谁？
> _dōu yǒu shéi?_
> **B:** 爸爸、妈妈、哥哥、妹妹和我。
> _bàba, māma, gēge, mèimei hé wǒ._
> **A:** 你哥哥多大？
> _nǐ gēge duō dà?_
> **B:** 他今年二十岁。
> _tā jīnnián èrshí suì._

## Dialogue 2 — What your family does

> **A:** 你爸爸做什么工作？
> _nǐ bàba zuò shénme gōngzuò?_
> **B:** 他是老师。
> _tā shì lǎoshī._
> **A:** 你妈妈呢？
> _nǐ māma ne?_
> **B:** 她是医生。
> _tā shì yīshēng._
> **A:** 你妹妹也工作吗？
> _nǐ mèimei yě gōngzuò ma?_
> **B:** 她不工作，她是学生。
> _tā bù gōngzuò, tā shì xuésheng._

## Dialogue 3 — A small family, and trading back

> **A:** 请问，你家有几口人？
> _qǐngwèn, nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ kǒu rén?_
> **B:** 我家有三口人：爸爸、妈妈和我。
> _wǒ jiā yǒu sān kǒu rén: bàba, māma hé wǒ._
> **A:** 你没有哥哥姐姐吗？
> _nǐ méi yǒu gēge jiějie ma?_
> **B:** 没有。你呢？
> _méi yǒu. nǐ ne?_
> **A:** 我家有五口人。我有两个妹妹。
> _wǒ jiā yǒu wǔ kǒu rén. wǒ yǒu liǎng gè mèimei._
> **B:** 她们多大？
> _tā men duō dà?_
> **A:** 十岁和八岁。
> _shí suì hé bā suì._
> **B:** 谢谢！再见。
> _xièxie! zàijiàn._

## Notes for the sketch generator

- Open with a family-size question (你家有几口人？) or a greeting that invites one.
- Mid-arc beats: name the members (爸爸 / 妈妈 / 哥哥 / 妹妹 + 和), ask an age
  with 多大 answered by number + 岁, then jobs with 做什么工作 answered by 是 + noun.
- Bounce with 呢 to hand the question back — it is the cheapest way to keep the
  learner talking.
- Withhold ages and the family count until the learner asks; those are the
  request slots (`topic.md`).

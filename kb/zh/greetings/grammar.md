# Grammar — Greetings (你好)

Minimal patterns to greet, name yourself, ask how someone is, and close. Each
pattern uses only `vocab.md` words.

## 1. Greeting formula — Pronoun + 好

好 ("good") plus a pronoun is the all-purpose greeting. No verb needed.

- 你好。 — Hello.
- 您好。 — Hello. (polite, e.g. to 老师)
- 早上好。 — Good morning. (time word + 好)

## 2. Subject + 很 + Adjective

Chinese adjectives act as predicates with **no 是**. 很 is the default,
near-empty linker before a one-syllable adjective.

- 我很好。 — I'm fine.
- 我也很好。 — I'm fine too. (也 goes before 很)

> Common learner error: inserting 是 → ✗ 我是好. Adjective predicates take 很,
> not 是.

## 3. Yes/no questions with 吗

Statement + 吗 turns it into a yes/no question. Word order does not change.

- 你好吗？ — How are you? (lit. "you good 吗?")
- 你是老师吗？ — Are you a teacher?

Answer by repeating/negating the verb or adjective: 我很好。 / 不，我是同学。

## 4. Follow-up questions with 呢

Noun/pronoun + 呢 bounces a just-asked question back. Avoids repeating the
whole sentence.

- 我很好，你呢？ — I'm good, and you?

## 5. Names — 叫 and 姓

- 叫 gives the full name or given name: 我叫小明。 / 你叫什么名字？
- 姓 gives the surname only: 我姓王。 / 您贵姓？ (set polite form; here use 你姓什么？)

## 6. Identity with 是

是 links two nouns (A 是 B). Unlike adjectives, identity *does* use 是.

- 他是老师。 — He is a teacher.
- 这是我朋友。 — This is my friend.

## 7. Set phrase — 认识你很高兴

A fixed "nice to meet you." Both orders are common:

- 认识你很高兴。 / 很高兴认识你。 — (I'm) glad to meet you.

## 8. Apologies / acknowledgement

- 对不起。 — Sorry.
- 没关系。 — It's okay. (the standard reply to 对不起 / 谢谢)

# Seed dialogues — Greetings (你好)

Short exchanges that feed the session **sketch** (per `DESIGN.md`). They model
the arc — greet → names → how-are-you → close — using only `vocab.md` words plus
proper names (王, 李, 小明). Lines are short on purpose (beginner disfluency
mitigation: the partner asks questions expecting 2–4 word answers).

Each line shows 汉字 then **pinyin** (the same format the partner reply uses).
Pinyin is generated by `_tools/annotate_pinyin.py` from the curated `vocab.md`
readings, so it always agrees with the vocabulary table (neutral tones, 不/一
sandhi). Regenerate after editing a line.

## Dialogue 1 — Meeting for the first time

> **A:** 你好！
> _nǐ hǎo_
> **B:** 你好！
> _nǐ hǎo_
> **A:** 我叫小明。你叫什么名字？
> _wǒ jiào xiǎomíng. nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?_
> **B:** 我叫小王。
> _wǒ jiào xiǎowáng._
> **A:** 认识你很高兴。
> _rènshi nǐ hěn gāoxìng._
> **B:** 认识你很高兴。
> _rènshi nǐ hěn gāoxìng._

## Dialogue 2 — How are you / and you?

> **A:** 你好吗？
> _nǐ hǎo ma?_
> **B:** 我很好，谢谢。你呢？
> _wǒ hěn hǎo, xièxie. nǐ ne?_
> **A:** 我也很好。
> _wǒ yě hěn hǎo._
> **B:** 你最近怎么样？
> _nǐ zuìjìn zěnmeyàng?_
> **A:** 很好！
> _hěn hǎo!_

## Dialogue 3 — Polite, with a teacher, and closing

> **A:** 老师，您好！
> _lǎoshī, nín hǎo!_
> **B:** 你好！
> _nǐ hǎo!_
> **A:** 这是我朋友，他姓李。
> _zhè shì wǒ péngyou, tā xìng lǐ._
> **B:** 你好！认识你很高兴。
> _nǐ hǎo! rènshi nǐ hěn gāoxìng._
> **C:** 您好！
> _nín hǎo!_
> **B:** 好，再见！
> _hǎo, zàijiàn!_
> **A:** 老师再见！
> _lǎoshī zàijiàn!_

## Notes for the sketch generator

- Open with 你好 / 您好 (pick polite if the partner is framed as 老师).
- Mid-arc beats: exchange names (叫 / 姓 / 名字), then a 你好吗 / 最近怎么样
  check-in answered with 很 + adjective and bounced back with 呢.
- Close on 再见 once `target_turns` is approached.

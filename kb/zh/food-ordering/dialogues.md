# Seed dialogues — Ordering food (点菜)

Short exchanges that feed the session **sketch** (per `DESIGN.md`). They model
the arc — sit down → ask what is good → ask what there is to drink → order →
pay and close — using only `vocab.md` words. Lines are short on purpose
(beginner disfluency mitigation: the server asks questions expecting 2–4 word
answers).

Each line shows 汉字 then **pinyin** (the same format the partner reply uses).
Pinyin is generated by `_tools/annotate_pinyin.py` from the curated `vocab.md`
readings, so it always agrees with the vocabulary table (neutral tones, 不/一
sandhi). Regenerate after editing a line.

**A** is the server, **B** is the learner.

## Dialogue 1 — Asking what is good here

> **A:** 你好！这是菜单。
> _nǐ hǎo! zhè shì càidān._
> **B:** 谢谢。请问，什么菜最好吃？
> _xièxie. qǐngwèn, shénme cài zuì hǎochī?_
> **A:** 鱼最好吃，饺子也很好吃。
> _yú zuì hǎochī, jiǎozi yě hěn hǎochī._
> **B:** 好，我要鱼。
> _hǎo, wǒ yào yú._

## Dialogue 2 — Asking what there is to drink

> **A:** 你要喝什么？
> _nǐ yào hē shénme?_
> **B:** 你们有什么？
> _nǐ men yǒu shénme?_
> **A:** 有茶，有水，也有牛奶。
> _yǒu chá, yǒu shuǐ, yě yǒu niúnǎi._
> **B:** 请给我一杯茶。
> _qǐng gěi wǒ yī bēi chá._
> **A:** 好。还要别的吗？
> _hǎo. hái yào biéde ma?_
> **B:** 不要了，谢谢。
> _bù yào le, xièxie._

## Dialogue 3 — Ordering and paying

> **A:** 你想吃什么？
> _nǐ xiǎng chī shénme?_
> **B:** 我想吃包子。请问，一个包子多少钱？
> _wǒ xiǎng chī bāozi. qǐngwèn, yī gè bāozi duōshao qián?_
> **A:** 两块。
> _liǎng kuài._
> **B:** 我要三个包子和两碗米饭。
> _wǒ yào sān gè bāozi hé liǎng wǎn mǐfàn._
> **A:** 好。三十块。
> _hǎo. sān shí kuài._
> **B:** 谢谢，再见！
> _xièxie, zàijiàn!_

## Notes for the sketch generator

- Open as the server: greet, hand over the 菜单, ask 你要点什么？
- Mid-arc beats: a recommendation (什么菜最好吃 → 鱼 / 饺子 / 包子), then the
  drinks list (茶, 水, 牛奶), then the order with a number + measure word.
- Withhold both: state no dish as best and no drink list until the learner asks.
  Pour nothing, name nothing — leave the order pad open.
- Close on 再见 once the order is placed.

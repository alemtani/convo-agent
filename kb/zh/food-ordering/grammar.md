# Grammar — Ordering food (点菜)

Minimal patterns to ask what is good, ask what there is, order, and pay. Each
pattern uses only `vocab.md` words.

## 1. Ordering — 要 / 想 / 点 / 来

Four ways to say what you want. All take the same word order: Subject + verb +
Number + Measure + Noun.

- 我要一碗米饭。 — I want a bowl of rice.
- 我想吃鱼。 — I'd like to eat fish. (想 + verb = "would like to")
- 我点两个菜。 — I'll order two dishes.
- 来一杯茶。 — I'll have a cup of tea. (来 replaces the verb; very common in a 饭馆)

> 想 needs a verb after it: ✓ 我想喝茶 / ✗ 我想茶.

## 2. Measure words — 个, 杯, 碗

A number never touches a noun directly. Put a measure word between them.

- 一个包子 — one steamed bun (个 is the default)
- 两杯水 — two cups of water (杯 for cups/glasses)
- 三碗米饭 — three bowls of rice (碗 for bowls)

> Use 两, not 二, before a measure word: ✓ 两杯茶 / ✗ 二杯茶. 二 is for counting
> and for numbers like 二十.

## 3. Asking what there is — 有什么 / 有…吗

有 means "there is". It is the question form for a menu.

- 你们有什么菜？ — What dishes do you have?
- 有茶吗？ — Do you have tea?

Answer with 有 or 没有: 有，我们有茶。 / 没有。

## 4. Asking what is good — 最 + adjective

最 means "most". Put it before the adjective.

- 什么菜最好吃？ — Which dish is the tastiest?
- 这个菜很好吃。 — This dish is very tasty.

Adjective predicates take 很, not 是: ✗ 这个菜是好吃.

## 5. Anything else — 还要别的吗

The server's standard follow-up. 还 = "still / in addition", 别的 = "other".

- 还要别的吗？ — Anything else?
- 不要了，谢谢。 — Nothing else, thanks. (了 marks the change: I'm done ordering)

## 6. Asking the price — 多少钱

- 多少钱？ — How much?
- 请问，一碗米饭多少钱？ — Excuse me, how much is a bowl of rice?
- 二十块。 — Twenty kuai.

## 7. Politeness — 请问 and 谢谢

请问 opens a question to a stranger. 谢谢 closes an exchange; 再见 closes the
session.

- 请问，什么菜最好吃？ — Excuse me, which dish is the tastiest?

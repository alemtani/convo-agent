# Grammar — Numbers and money (多少钱)

Minimal patterns to count, ask a price, react to a price, and say how many you
want. Each pattern uses only `vocab.md` words.

## 1. Numbers 1–99

Numbers build from ten pieces. 十 is "ten"; a digit **before** 十 multiplies it,
a digit **after** 十 adds to it.

- 十五 — 15 (ten + five)
- 二十 — 20 (two × ten)
- 五十五 — 55 (five × ten + five)
- 一百 — 100

## 2. 二 or 两

Both mean "two". 二 counts; 两 goes before a measure word.

- 二十块 — 20 kuai.
- 两个水果 — two pieces of fruit.

> Common learner error: ✗ 二个水果. Before a measure word, use 两.

## 3. Number + measure word + noun

A number never touches a noun directly. A measure word sits between them.

- 三个水果 — three pieces of fruit. (个 — general)
- 两本书 — two books. (本 — books)
- 一杯茶 — one cup of tea. (杯 — drinks)
- 一件衣服 — one piece of clothing. (件 — clothes)

Drop the noun once it is known: 我要两个。 — "I want two."

## 4. Asking the price — 多少钱

多少 asks "how much". The thing comes first.

- 这个多少钱？ — How much is this one?
- 水果多少钱？ — How much is the fruit?
- 一共多少钱？ — How much in total?

## 5. Saying a price — 块 and 毛

块 is the spoken unit; 元 is the written one. 毛 is one tenth of a 块.

- 十块。 — 10 kuai.
- 二十五块。 — 25 kuai.
- 五块八毛。 — 5 kuai 80.

## 6. 太 + adjective + 了

太 means "too much of". 了 closes the pattern and is not optional here.

- 太贵了。 — Too expensive.
- 太大了。 — Too big.

> Common learner error: dropping 了 → ✗ 太贵. Use 很 for a plain statement
> (很贵 — "expensive") and 太…了 for a complaint.

## 7. 有没有 — asking whether something exists

Verb + 没 + verb makes a yes/no question without 吗.

- 有没有便宜的？ — Do you have a cheaper one?
- 有没有小的？ — Do you have a small one?

Answer 有。 or 没有。

## 8. 的 turns an adjective into "the … one"

Adjective + 的 stands in for the noun, so you never have to repeat it.

- 便宜的 — the cheap one.
- 小一点儿的 — a slightly smaller one.
- 红的 — the red one.
- 我要那个红的。 — I want that red one.

## 9. Colours

Colour + 的 works like any other adjective. 什么颜色 asks which colour.

- 这个包是红的。 — This bag is red.
- 你要什么颜色的？ — What colour do you want?

## 10. Saying what you want — 要 and 买

要 states the order; 买 names the act of buying.

- 我要两个。 — I'll take two.
- 我要买书。 — I want to buy a book.
- 我不要那个。 — I don't want that one.

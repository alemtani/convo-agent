# Seed dialogues — Self-introduction (自我介绍)

Short exchanges that feed the session **sketch** (per `DESIGN.md`). They model
the arc — country → where you live → work or study → languages → age → close —
using only `vocab.md` words plus the declared proper names (上海, 美国, 英国,
小明, 小王, 小李). Lines are short on purpose (beginner disfluency mitigation:
the partner asks questions expecting 2–4 word answers).

Each line shows 汉字 then **pinyin** (the same format the partner reply uses).
Pinyin is generated by `_tools/annotate_pinyin.py` from the curated `vocab.md`
readings, so it always agrees with the vocabulary table (neutral tones, 不/一
sandhi). Regenerate after editing a line.

## Dialogue 1 — Where are you from

> **A:** 你好！请问，你是哪国人？
> _nǐ hǎo qǐngwèn nǐ shì nǎ guó rén_
> **B:** 我是中国人，我从上海来。你呢？
> _wǒ shì Zhōngguó rén wǒ cóng shànghǎi lái nǐ ne_
> **A:** 我是美国人。
> _wǒ shì měiguó rén_
> **B:** 你住在哪儿？
> _nǐ zhù zài nǎr_
> **A:** 我住在北京。
> _wǒ zhù zài Běijīng_

## Dialogue 2 — Work and study

> **A:** 你做什么工作？
> _nǐ zuò shénme gōngzuò_
> **B:** 我是老师，我在大学工作。你呢？
> _wǒ shì lǎoshī wǒ zài dàxué gōngzuò nǐ ne_
> **A:** 我不上班。我是大学生。
> _wǒ bù shàngbān wǒ shì dàxuéshēng_
> **B:** 你学习什么？
> _nǐ xuéxí shénme_
> **A:** 我在学校学习汉语。
> _wǒ zài xuéxiào xuéxí Hànyǔ_
> **B:** 你的汉语很好！
> _nǐ de Hànyǔ hěn hǎo_
> **A:** 谢谢！
> _xièxie_

## Dialogue 3 — Languages, age, and closing

> **A:** 你会说外语吗？
> _nǐ huì shuō wàiyǔ ma_
> **B:** 我会说一点儿英语。你呢？
> _wǒ huì shuō yìdiǎnr Yīngyǔ nǐ ne_
> **A:** 我会说汉语和英语。
> _wǒ huì shuō Hànyǔ hé Yīngyǔ_
> **B:** 你今年几岁？
> _nǐ jīnnián jǐ suì_
> **A:** 我今年二十岁。你呢？
> _wǒ jīnnián èrshí suì nǐ ne_
> **B:** 我也是二十岁。
> _wǒ yě shì èrshí suì_
> **A:** 认识你很高兴。再见！
> _rènshi nǐ hěn gāoxìng zàijiàn_
> **B:** 再见！
> _zàijiàn_

## Notes for the sketch generator

- Open with 你好 plus one fact about the partner's own situation, so the learner
  has something to answer and something to ask back.
- Mid-arc beats: country (哪国人 / 从…来), place of living (住在哪儿), then work
  or study (做什么工作 / 学习什么). Languages and age are optional extras once the
  four slots are moving.
- Withhold the partner's country and job until the learner asks — both are
  `request` slots (`docs/SCENARIOS.md`).
- Close on 再见 once the goal is met or the turn cap is near.

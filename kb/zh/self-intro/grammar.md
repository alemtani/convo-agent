# Grammar — Self-introduction (自我介绍)

Minimal patterns to say where you are from, what you do, what languages you
speak, and how old you are. Each pattern uses only `vocab.md` words.

## 1. Identity with 是 — A 是 B

是 links two nouns. Nationality and job are both noun phrases, so both use 是.

- 我是中国人。 — I am Chinese.
- 他是老师。 — He is a teacher.
- 我是大学生。 — I am a university student.

Negate with 不 before 是: 我不是老师。

## 2. Asking nationality — 哪国人

哪 ("which") replaces the answer word. Word order does not change.

- 你是哪国人？ — What country are you from?
- 我是中国人。 — I am Chinese.

## 3. 从 … 来 — coming from a place

从 marks the origin; 来 is the verb and comes last.

- 我从北京来。 — I come from Beijing.
- 你从哪儿来？ — Where do you come from?

> Common learner error: putting 来 before the place → ✗ 我来从北京. The place
> follows 从, and the verb ends the phrase.

## 4. 在 + place + verb — where you live, study, or work

在 marks location and goes **before** the verb, not after it.

- 我住在北京。 — I live in Beijing. (住 takes 在 after it — a set exception)
- 我在大学工作。 — I work at a university.
- 我在学校学习汉语。 — I study Chinese at school.
- 你住在哪儿？ — Where do you live?

## 5. Asking about a job — 做什么工作

做 ("to do") plus 什么工作 asks for the job itself.

- 你做什么工作？ — What work do you do?
- 我是医生。 — I am a doctor.
- 我不上班，我是学生。 — I don't work; I am a student.

## 6. 会 + verb — ability learned through study

会 means "know how to". It goes before the verb.

- 我会说汉语。 — I can speak Chinese.
- 你会说外语吗？ — Can you speak a foreign language?
- 我会说一点儿英语。 — I can speak a little English. (一点儿 follows the verb)

## 7. Age — 今年几岁

岁 is the counter itself, so no other measure word appears.

- 你今年几岁？ — How old are you this year?
- 我今年二十岁。 — I am twenty this year.

## 8. Linking with 和 and 也

- 和 joins nouns only, never two clauses: 我会说汉语和英语。
- 也 joins clauses and sits before the verb: 我也是学生。

Convo Agent

Speak or type Mandarin (greetings). A real partner replies and remembers the conversation; spoken words come back with per-syllable tone scores (green good / amber ok / red off). The partner speaks its reply instead of writing it — that's the listening practice. Tap 🔊 to hear a line again, 👁 to see the words when you're stuck, or 👁 in the bar below to show every reply's text. Tap ⌨️ to type in pinyin instead — your words come back as 汉字. Add tone numbers (ni3hao3) and your tones get checked too.

Speak or type Mandarin (greetings). A real partner replies and remembers the conversation; spoken words come back with per-syllable tone scores (green good / amber ok / red off). The partner speaks its reply instead of writing it — that's the listening practice. Tap 🔊 to hear a line again, 👁 Show text to see the words when you're stuck, or 👁 Show all text below to reveal every reply at once. If you're stuck for good, I'm stuck ends the session and writes your feedback — that is a fine way to finish one. Under ⋯: ⌨️ Type instead lets you type pinyin — your words come back as 汉字 — and 🔀 Try something else starts a different scenario. Add tone numbers (ni3hao3) and your tones get checked too.
